Story

It started as a joke in a bar

The Saratov password finally has a form you can carry with you.

In the summer of 2025 we were sitting in a bar in Tbilisi, trying to think of something every person from Saratov knows — and no one else does. Around us was the usual émigré life: people from different cities and countries, scattered across Georgia, Serbia, Armenia, Israel. Pretty quickly we landed on nazook.

It turned out to be an almost perfect Saratov password. To most people the word “nazook” means nothing at all. But anyone from Saratov — in Tbilisi, London, Paris, San Francisco — recognises it instantly. And with it, they recognise their own.

There was only one problem: you cannot really carry nazook around. It is pastry after all, and it survives borders and continents worse than people do. So we ran with the idea: let’s make a pin.

Back then it felt more like a good prank than a project. But the prank got out of hand fast: first sketches, new versions of the design, looking for makers, experiments with production, logistics and a dozen other things we had no idea about that evening.

A year later, at the end of the summer of 2026, we are finally releasing it.
